A PCTC is a box with a ramp. That is not a criticism: the box is the most efficient shape for carrying vehicles, and the ramp is why it takes hours, not days, to load. Everything that makes the class useful to a car maker makes it useful to an army.

## Civil reading

The fleet is small — a few hundred deep-sea ships — and until recently was old. A wave of Chinese vehicle exports drove a newbuild boom and record charter rates, then a correction. Read the class through the export figures and the orderbook.

## Military reading

Most strategic sealift arrangements list PCTCs and other ro-ros first. What limits their use is port infrastructure at the destination, the availability of stevedoring, and whether a civilian crew will sail into a contested area. Those are the questions a dossier should prompt — not "can it carry tanks?", which it can.
