**Where we are.** Storage (<Sym s="EU.GASSTOR" />) is comfortably above the seasonal norm, LNG send-out is steady, and the front of the curve has drifted lower through the summer.

**What could break it.** Two things at once: a below-normal temperature run of three weeks or more, and a supply interruption of any kind — a Norwegian outage, a chokepoint incident, a cargo diversion east on a JKM spike. Either alone is absorbed. Together they are not.

**What we are watching.** The JKM–TTF spread, the LNG freight rate (<Sym s="LNGF.174" />), and the pace of withdrawals once they begin. A withdrawal rate that runs ahead of the five-year curve for two consecutive weeks is the trigger to change this stance.
