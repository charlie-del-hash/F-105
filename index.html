Headlines

  1. NRG·brief17 Sept
    TTF: storage is full, the risk is in the flow
    European storage entered autumn above the five-year average. That is a cushion, not a hedge — a cold winter and a supply interruption together would still drain it by February.
  2. AIR·article16 Sept
    Thud Ridge: how a nuclear bomber became the workhorse of Rolling Thunder
    The F-105 was designed to run a single tactical nuke to a Soviet target at low level. Instead it flew three-quarters of the strike missions against North Vietnam — and lost nearly half its production run doing it.
  3. SHP·brief15 Sept
    VLCC: the premium is in the ballast leg
    TD3C has firmed without a matching move in cargo volumes. Owners are pricing the risk of waiting empty inside the Gulf — the ballast leg — rather than the laden transit out.
  4. GEO·dossier·Hormuz14 Sept
    Strait of Hormuz
    The 21-nautical-mile gap between Iran and the Musandam peninsula through which roughly a fifth of the world's traded oil and LNG passes. The single most consequential chokepoint for the energy and freight desks.
  5. SHP·article12 Sept
    The long way round: what the Red Sea diversions did to the box market
    When container lines stopped transiting Bab el-Mandeb, they did not lose ships — they lost weeks. The maths of a 10-to-14-day detour explains almost everything that followed in rates, schedules and port congestion.
  6. AIR·dossier·F-105D / F-105G10 Sept
    Republic F-105 Thunderchief
    Single-seat supersonic strike fighter; the USAF's primary conventional bomber over North Vietnam and the origin of the Wild Weasel SEAD mission.
  7. SHP·article08 Sept
    Every ro-ro is a sealift ship
    Strategic sealift is not a navy. It is a car carrier under charter, a ferry with a strengthened deck, an LNG tanker whose route is a matter of state. Reading the merchant fleet as a military asset is the habit this journal exists to teach.
  8. SHP·dossier·PCTC, 6,500–8,000 CEU06 Sept
    Pure car and truck carrier (PCTC)
    The ro-ro that moves the world's cars is also the ro-ro that would move an armoured brigade. A vessel class whose civilian and military readings are almost the same ship.
